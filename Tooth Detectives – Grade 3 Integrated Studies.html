2 · What Do Teeth Do?

God made us with different types of teeth. Our teeth work like tools to cut, tear, crush, grind and chew food. Tap a tooth to learn more.

Our Teeth Song (tune: Are You Sleeping)
Eight incisors, and eight molars. Four canine, four canine. We use them when we eat, to cut, tear, chew.
Try it! Touch your teeth with your tongue. Some feel pointy and sharp. Some feel flat with ridges. Using our teeth to cut and chew food makes it easier to swallow.
