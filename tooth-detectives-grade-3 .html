🦷 2. Sets of Teeth

Humans grow two sets of teeth. The first set has 20 teeth. They are called baby teeth, primary teeth or temporary teeth. Temporary means only for a short time. The second set has 32 teeth. They are called permanent teeth, or adult teeth.
